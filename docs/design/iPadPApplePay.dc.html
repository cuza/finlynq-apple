Log Apple Pay purchases

Every time you pay with Apple Pay, the store and amount go straight to Finlynq. Purchases wait in Review, so your bank import links to them instead of adding them twice.

Not set up yet. Takes about a minute in the Shortcuts app.

In Shortcuts

  1. 1Tap Automation, then +.
  2. 2Choose Wallet and pick the cards to log.
  3. 3Select Run Immediately, then Next.
  4. 4Search Finlynq and add Add Apple Pay purchase.
  5. 5Tap each blue field and pick Merchant, Amount and Card from Shortcut Input. Tap Done.
Wait until I unlock Finlynq

When off, Finlynq signs in in the background to save the purchase.
